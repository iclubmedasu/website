# iClub Platform — User Guide

Welcome to **iClub, MED-ASU** — the Innovation Club at the Faculty of Medicine, Ain Shams University.

This guide explains what you can do on the platform from a **user's point of view**. It is written for club members, event attendees, and anyone exploring the public website — not for developers.

---

## Two parts of the platform

| Part | Who it's for | What you do there |
|------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Public website** | Everyone (no login needed) | Browse events, register, view projects and members, contact the club, verify certificates |
| **Members portal** | Invited club members only | Manage your profile, teams, projects, events, certificates, and more |

---

## Part 1 — Public website (for everyone)

Open the public website from your browser. Use the top navigation bar to move between pages.

### Home

The home page introduces iClub with a hero section, highlights, and previews of upcoming events and recent projects. It is a good starting point if you want a quick overview of what the club is doing right now.

### Events

**Browse events:** Go to **Events** in the navigation to see published club events. Click any event to open its detail page with description, date, location, sessions, and photo gallery.

**Register for an event:**

1. Open the event you want to attend.
2. Click **Register** (or follow the registration link shared with you).
3. Fill in your details — name, email, and any custom questions the event organizer added.
4. Choose a ticket tier or session if the event offers multiple options.
5. Submit the form. You will receive a confirmation and, when applicable, a ticket by email.

**After registering:**

- Use the **Join** link from your ticket email when it is time for the event (especially for online or hybrid sessions).
- Open the **Confirmation** page to review your registration details.

**Share an event:** On an event detail page, use the share options to copy a link you can send to others.

### Projects

Go to **Projects** to browse club projects that have been made public. Each project page shows a summary of what the team worked on. This is a great way to see past and ongoing initiatives.

### Members directory

Go to **Members** to browse club members who have chosen to appear publicly. Click a member to view their profile, role history, and achievements (based on their privacy settings).

### About

The **About** page tells the story of iClub — mission, values, sections, and sponsors. Content here is managed by club officers.

### Contact

The **Contact** page lists ways to reach the club (email, social links, etc.). You can also send a message through the contact form; it goes directly to the club's inbox.

### Support

The **Support** page is where you can:

- Read important notices from the club (available in **English and Arabic**).
- Submit an **incident report** if something went wrong (for example, a registration issue or a problem at an event).

Fill in the form with as much detail as you can. The club team will follow up as needed.

### Verify a certificate

If you received a certificate from iClub, you can verify it publicly:

1. Open the verification link you were given, or go to the **Verify** page with your certificate code.
2. The page shows the certificate details and lets you view or download it.

This confirms the certificate is genuine and was issued by iClub.

## Part 2 — Members portal (for club members)

The members portal is a private workspace for people who belong to iClub. You need an invitation to join — there is no open self-signup.

### Logging in for the first time

1. Go to the members portal login page.
2. Enter the **email**, **student ID**, or **phone number** you were invited with.
3. If this is your first time:
   - Complete your profile (name, contact details, student ID if applicable).
   - Create a password that meets the requirements (at least 8 characters, with uppercase, lowercase, a number, and a symbol).
4. If you already have an account, enter your password and sign in.

After login, you land on the main portal with a sidebar on the left (or a menu button on mobile).

### Who can access what?

Not every member sees every page. Access depends on your role and assignment status:

| Your status | What you can access |
|-------------|---------------------|
| **Invited but not yet assigned to a team** | Your profile page only, until an officer assigns you |
| **Alumni** | Your profile page only |
| **Regular assigned member** | Dashboard, Personnel, Projects, Events, Help & Support |
| **Team leadership** (Head / Vice) | Everything above, plus **Documents** and **Certificates** |
| **Fundraising Head/Vice** | Everything above, plus **Finance** (view) |
| **Officers / President / VP / Developers** | Full access including Documents, site editors, Administration, Finance, and Certificates |

If you try to open a page you do not have access to, it simply will not appear in your sidebar.

### Your profile

Click your **avatar** at the bottom of the sidebar to open **My Profile**. Your profile has several tabs:

| Tab | What you can do |
|-----|-----------------|
| **Personal Details** | View and edit your name, contact info, student ID, and profile photo |
| **Privacy** | Choose what contact details other members can see on your public profile |
| **Sign-in & Security** | Change your password |
| **Notifications** | See alerts about tasks, events, projects, and other club activity |
| **History** | View your role history across teams |
| **Achievements** | See certificates and achievements linked to your account |

**Notifications badge:** The bell icon in the sidebar footer shows how many unread notifications you have. Click it to jump straight to your notifications tab.

### Install the portal on your phone (PWA)

The members portal can be installed like an app:

- On **Android/Chrome**: tap the browser menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- On **iPhone/Safari**: tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

Once installed, you can open iClub from your home screen and use it offline for basic pages.

### Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is your home base after login. It shows:

- Quick stats about your involvement
- **My Tasks** — tasks assigned to you across projects and events
- **Upcoming Events** — events coming up soon
- A feed of recent **Notifications**

Use the dashboard to see what needs your attention today.

### Personnel

Under **Personnel** in the sidebar:

| Page | What it is |
|------|------------|
| **Teams** | Browse all club teams, their roles, subteams, and members |
| **Members** | Search and view all active members; open a member's full profile |
| **Alumni** | View members who have moved to alumni status |
| **Administration** | Manage club officers and leadership (officers only) |

On the **Teams** page you can explore team structure, see who holds which role, and view member assignments.

On the **Members** page you can filter the directory and click any member to see their profile, teams, and history.

### Projects

Under **Projects**:

| Page | What it is |
|------|------------|
| **Active Projects** | Ongoing club projects |
| **Past Projects** | Completed or archived projects |

Click a project to expand it and work with:

- **Phases and tasks** — the work breakdown for the project
- **Gantt chart / schedule** — timeline view of tasks and deadlines
- **Files** — upload and download project documents
- **Comments** — discuss tasks and files with your team
- **Activity log** — see what changed and when
- **Certificates** — issue certificates to eligible participants (leadership+)

Project leads can create projects, add phases and tasks, assign members, and move projects through their lifecycle (active → on hold → finalized → archived).

### Documents

Go to **Documents** to browse club folders, root (uncategorized) files, and open documents you have access to. Regular members do not see Documents — only org leadership and team Heads/Vice.

**Who can see what (two tiers)**

| Tier | Who | Default visibility |
|------|-----|--------------------|
| Org leadership | Developer, Officer, President, Vice President | All documents and folders (admin override) |
| Team leadership | Head / Vice of a team | Only documents/folders scoped to their team(s), unless granted |

Ownership is positional and live: a document or folder is owned by whoever currently holds the position tied to its team scope.

- **Team-scoped** (`scopeTeamId` set): that team's current Head/Vice own it. Org leadership (Officer / President / VP / developers) can always view and manage as an admin override.
- **Org-owned** (`scopeTeamId` empty): only org leadership has natural access.

Team-scoped items are exclusive to that team's Head/Vice (plus org leadership). Other teams do not see them unless explicitly granted access.

**Browsing**

- Root view shows **Folders** (3-row scroll) and **Root documents** (files not in a folder).
- Open a **folder** to see its documents as tiles.
- **Locked** documents and folders show a lock badge and a **Request access** control — click to request access (optionally with a note). Cross-team Heads/Vice see locks; Head and Vice of the item's own team share access and are not locked.
- A **file grant** unlocks the parent folder for the granted team, but inside they see **only** the granted files (other files are hidden, not shown as locked). A **folder grant** shows **all** files in that folder (view-only).
- Open a document you can access to view details and **Download** it.
- Review pending **Access Requests** (documents and folders) whenever you can manage items you own. Empty when nothing is waiting for you to approve.

**Uploading and folders**

Anyone with upload rights (org or team leadership) can:

- **Upload** at root (no folder) — multi-file upload is supported
- **Upload into a folder** only if they **own** that folder (positional team scope) or are org leadership — grant recipients cannot upload into folders they were only granted access to
- **Create** folders scoped to a team (team Heads/Vice create under their led team; org leadership may leave scope empty for org-owned folders)
- **Rename or delete** only folders they own (org leadership can manage any folder; folders that still contain documents cannot be deleted)
- **Drag** a root document onto a folder tile to move it (destination must be a folder they can manage)

Inside a folder there is a single **Upload** control in the toolbar (empty folders show hint text only, not a second button). Upload appears only for folder owners / org leadership.

**Granting access**

Owners only — org leadership for any item, or team leadership for documents/folders in their scope — can:

- **Grant access** to a **team** for a set duration (day, week, month, or indefinite). A team grant covers that team's **Head and Vice** together — not all members, and not a single person.
- **Grant folder access** — the same TEAM rule; Head/Vice of the granted team can view and download every document in that folder (including future uploads while the folder grant remains)
- **Manage access** on a folder (overflow menu) or revoke grants from the document detail **Access** list — review active TEAM grants
- Approve or deny pending access requests (approval creates a **TEAM** grant for each team the requester currently leads as Head/Vice, with the chosen duration)

Grant recipients (Head/Vice via TEAM grants) can **view and download only** — they cannot rename, delete, upload into the folder, grant further, or manage access.

Document grants apply to that file only (and unlock the parent folder with a partial listing). Folder grants apply to all files in the folder.

**Access logs**

- Document access log (who viewed or downloaded a file): document owners (positional scope) or org leadership — not grant-only viewers, and not based on who originally uploaded the file
- Folder access log: available from the folder overflow menu for members who can manage folder access

### Events

Under **Events**:

| Page | What it is |
|------|------------|
| **Events** | Active and upcoming club events |
| **Past Events** | Completed or archived events |

**Creating an event** (leadership+): click **New Event** and fill in title, dates, location, tiers, custom registration fields, and sessions.

**Managing an event:** open any event to access:

| Section | What you can do |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Overview** | Edit details, publish to the public website, share the public link |
| **Tiers & pricing** | Set ticket types and prices |
| **Sessions** | Configure time slots (including hybrid/online sessions) |
| **Registrations** | View, edit, import, or export the attendee list |
| **Check-in** | Scan QR codes or look up attendees at the door |
| **Tasks** | Assign and track event-day tasks with a timetable |
| **Tickets & reminders** | Send ticket emails and reminders to registrants |
| **Photos** | Upload and manage event photo galleries (shown on the public site) |
| **Certificates** | Issue certificates to eligible attendees |
| **Statistics** | See registration and attendance numbers |

**Check-in page:** go to an event's **Check-in** page during the event. Scan attendee QR codes from their tickets or search by name to mark attendance.

**Registrations page:** manage the full attendee table — edit contact details, change tiers or sessions, add walk-in registrations, and export to Excel.

### Help & Support

Under **General → Help & Support**:

- Read club notices and FAQs meant for members.
- Submit an **incident report** if you encounter a problem inside the portal (similar to the public Support page, but for members).

## Part 3 — For leaders and administration

These features appear in the sidebar only if your role allows it.

### Certificates

Go to **Certificates** to:

- View all issued certificates and their status (active, revoked, etc.)
- **Issue** a new certificate to a member (custom or from a template)
- **Revoke** or **reissue** an existing certificate
- **Resend** the certificate email

**Certificate templates** (leadership+):

- Go to **Certificates → New Template** to design a reusable certificate layout.
- Upload a background image, position text fields, and preview the result.
- Edit or deactivate templates from the templates list.

Issued certificates can be verified by anyone on the public website using the verification link or code.

### Finance

Go to **Finance** (officers and Fundraising leadership) to manage club finances:

- **Account balances** — see current balances across accounts
- **Transaction log** — record income and expenses
- **Liabilities** — track money owed
- **Scheduled items** — upcoming payments or expected income
- **Export** — download finance data to Excel

### Site content editors

Officers can edit what appears on the public website:

| Portal page | Public page it controls |
|-------------|-------------------------|
| **General → About** | `/about` |
| **General → Contact** | `/contact` |
| **General → Support page** | `/support` (notices + incident form settings) |

Changes you save in the portal appear on the public website after publishing.

### Administration

The **Administration** page (officers only) is where you manage club leadership:

- View and edit the Administration team roster
- Add or remove officers
- Perform **leadership handovers** when roles change (President, VP, officers)
- Manage admin team memberships

### Alumni management

Officers can move members to **Alumni** status from the Alumni page. Alumni retain access to their profile but cannot use the rest of the portal.

## Tips for your demo this week

1. **Start on the public website** — show Home, an event registration flow, and certificate verification. This is what most visitors see first.
2. **Switch to the members portal** — log in as a member and walk through Dashboard → a project → an event.
3. **Show role differences** — if possible, demo with two accounts (regular member vs. leadership) to show how the sidebar changes.
4. **Highlight the mobile experience** — open the portal on a phone and show the PWA install prompt.
5. **End with certificates** — issue a certificate in the portal, then verify it on the public site. This is a strong "wow" moment.
